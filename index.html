about

Foundation for Community Support Services (FOCUS) is a non-governmental organization that was formed in January 2000. It was initially established to tackle reproductive health issues affecting the youth (boys and girls), women and other vulnerable groups such as commercial sex workers. FOCUS got registered with Trustee Incorporation Act (1962) in September 2001; and with National Youth Council of Malawi under the National Youth Council (1996) Act in 2005. READ MORE

vision

envisages a self-reliant, healthy and resilient communities with improved quality of life that is aware and able to participate, demand and access their rights, information and services for meaningful development

mission

committed towards facilitating, advocating and implementing programs in Community Health; Youth Development; Gender and Women Empowerment; Good Governance; Human Rights; Sustainable Livelihood and Disaster Risk Reduction & Management targeting vulnerable Malawian communities.

values

transparency, professionalism, excellence, inclusiveness, commitment, integrity, honesty right based and accountability.
